# ADR-0016: Failure-first evidence before compositional question grammar

- Status: accepted design; G3FO implementation stopped indeterminate and unpromoted
- Date: 2026-08-23
- Requirements: RCI-073, RCI-074, RCI-077, and RCI-078

## Context

RCI preserves operational returns, predictions, mismatches, project-development
evidence, and manually authored capability limitations. The attempted G3FO slice sought
to add a pure deterministic bridge from an owned pre-return task protocol and the
authoritative effect lifecycle to a typed consequential result, unresolved failure
localization, and replayable cognitive handoff.

Three preserved proposals describe relations, open ports, dependent answer frames, and
bounded compositional question generation. They do not establish that a general algebra
is the present bottleneck. Adopting a replacement semantic kernel before external failure
evidence would allow the new grammar to validate itself through fixtures designed in its
own vocabulary.

## Decision

The G3FO candidate remains evidence, not standing implementation. Candidate head
`b3cb06363ac5b8cd60c9bfbda5c3fbdd7dca72fb` passed its complete local and hosted gates
after three invalid semantic reviews were repaired, but fresh distinct-context semantic
review was unavailable. PR #16 was closed without merge and its branch was retained.

RCI v0.5 and RCI-001--RCI-079 remain authoritative. The three exact source documents
under `docs/spec/sources/` are proposals and derivations, not live instructions. The
following correspondences are admissible candidates for a later bounded Goal:

- many-sorted relation signatures with an exact bound/open port partition;
- questions as typed open-relation fibers;
- field-relative complement and finite join-and-hide composition;
- fixed antecedent answer frames with applicability exterior, partial returns, and
  indeterminacy;
- answer-conditioned continuation represented as inert data;
- finite generation under explicit type, depth, size, port, and registry budgets;
- inert syntax reification and profile-relative productivity partitions.

The following collapses are rejected:

- candidate completions are not true relation members;
- semantic completion is not an independently captured external return;
- available observations, protected consequences, and attempted probes are distinct;
- profile-relative failure to split a fiber is not absolute equivalence without a
  complete independently certified context registry;
- question, probe, method, warrant, review, and promotion admission remain separate;
- learned probe admission is not question-signature or grammar extension;
- `InquiryState`, `ProbeTrace`, `RetentionPackage`, sealed event meanings, and the eight
  `core-v1` contracts are not replaced or reinterpreted;
- unrestricted quantification, arbitrary callables, free constructions, higher-order
  truth, broad operator algebra, and a v0.6 semantic rewrite remain deferred.

The recursive-generation result is conditional only: for a declared finite completion
carrier and a complete independently certified context registry, fair bounded
enumeration finds a separator whenever registered-context equivalence has multiple
classes. Without the completeness certificate, exhaustion returns `Unknown`.

## Consequences

No G3Q-F implementation or new stable requirement is admitted by this ADR. Selection
requires at least two distinct, owned, verified failure bindings showing that current
`QuestionContract`/G3Q shapes cannot express a consequentially necessary discriminator
or continuation. If selected later, implementation proceeds as three separate Goals:
finite dependent frames, answer-conditioned continuation, and only after two shared
implementations a bounded compositional kernel. Existing catalogs remain compatibility
profiles and generated expressions remain inert until independently admitted.
