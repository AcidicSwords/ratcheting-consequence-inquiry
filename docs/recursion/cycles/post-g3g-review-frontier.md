# Post-G3G review-route frontier inquiry

## Anchor and limitation

This bounded cycle begins from clean protected-main commit
`b6069c0a58a669eed21005bada407eff1828071a`, after G3G implementation and evidence
closeout. G1, G2A, G2B, G3A-H, G3R, G3Q, and G3G are standing verified capabilities.

The observed limitation is exact: G3R requires an exact-head reviewer context different
from the developer, but the record does not state which review relation ran or what
failure family it covered. A prior local Qwen review produced one schema-constrained
valid return; two later CLI review attempts were malformed and therefore
`Indeterminate`. Hosted CI independently executed the candidate but did not supply
open-ended semantic interpretation.

The protected difference is between (a) honestly retaining an unavailable or malformed
review as `Indeterminate`, and (b) obtaining reproducible bounded review evidence even
when every model route is disconnected. The cycle does not claim that finite mechanical
checking equals open-ended semantic review outside a declared fault family.

## Candidate frontier

The existing partial-order frontier was applied to five reversible successors. Cost
axes are bounded implementation inventories, not universal quality scores.

| Candidate | Typed gain | Missing seams | Authority-risk boundaries |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| bounded mechanical review equivalence | independent checkability | 1 | 0 |
| schema-constrained local-model breaker adapter | new method | 2 | 1 |
| fresh frontier-model reviewer route | scope extension | 1 | 1 |
| G3A-L exact linear binding | executable relation | 4 | 1 |
| isolated candidate actuator | development recovery | 4 | 5 |

Gain kinds differ, so the candidates remain nondominated. The bounded mechanical route
is the only candidate componentwise no worse on both declared costs. It is selected as
the smallest reversible discriminator, not as a total roadmap winner. Model adapters,
G3A-L, and actuation remain deferred frontier members.

## Selected discriminator

G3V asks whether an exact candidate/evidence bundle can receive the same expected
accept/reject/indeterminate disposition across a versioned seeded fault family while the
model route is absent. Its strongest positive result is `valid_within_profile`; semantic
residue outside the profile remains `Unknown`.

The implementation may add strict frozen review-contract, fault-observation,
assessment, and inert breaker-candidate records plus pure SDK/CLI inspection. It may not
change sealed `IndependentReview` semantics, satisfy promotion review requirements,
invoke a model, access the network, execute arbitrary commands, mutate source/Git, or
grant warrant.

## Reopening

Reopen when seeded fault coverage no longer matches the protected failure inventory, a
surviving mutant is observed, an admitted model breaker supplies a reproducible new
fault class, or evidence justifies a separately governed promotion-policy comparison.
