# Post-G3G review-route frontier inquiry

## Anchor and limitation

This cycle began from clean protected-main commit
`b6069c0a58a669eed21005bada407eff1828071a`. G3R required exact-head review from a
context distinct from the developer, while local secondary-model returns were sometimes
malformed and hosted CI supplied execution evidence rather than open-ended semantic
review.

The consequential distinction was between preserving unavailable review as `Unknown`
and obtaining reproducible evidence over a declared closed fault family. No finite
profile was permitted to claim open-ended semantic coverage.

## Candidate frontier

| Candidate | Typed gain | Missing seams | Authority-risk boundaries |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| bounded mechanical review equivalence | independent checkability | 1 | 0 |
| schema-constrained local-model breaker adapter | new method | 2 | 1 |
| fresh frontier-model reviewer route | scope extension | 1 | 1 |
| G3A-L exact linear binding | executable relation | 4 | 1 |
| isolated candidate actuator | development recovery | 4 | 5 |

The gain kinds were incomparable. The bounded mechanical route was selected as the
smallest reversible discriminator under these two inventory costs, not as a total
roadmap winner.

## Return and disposition

The G3V candidate passed its closed executable profile. Fresh semantic review was
unavailable, so the candidate could not pass the distinct G3R promotion relation and was
stopped indeterminate. This is evidence that deterministic checks can cover declared
fault families, not evidence that they are consequence-equivalent to an independent
semantic reviewer.

## Reopening

Reopen for a surviving seeded fault, a new reproducible breaker class, fresh independent
review, or a separately governed promotion-policy comparison.
