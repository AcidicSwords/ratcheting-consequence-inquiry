## Consequential boundary

representation, but it cannot construct or independently check the concrete linear
quotient promised by RCI-051--RCI-054. A valid exact rational binding would make those
theorems executable while preserving the existing authority stages. The incumbent and
successor therefore differ on exact protected consequences: rank, kernel, quotient
coordinates, finite-support almost-sure equivalence, and linear reopening are currently
unavailable and become independently decidable under the successor.
